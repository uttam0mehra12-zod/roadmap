📋 Challenge Overview

This comprehensive 90-day challenge is designed to transform you from a beginner into a proficient cybersecurity professional. Through 4 distinct phases, you'll master networking, cryptography, web security, penetration testing, and advanced exploitation techniques.

Daily Time Commitment: 5-7 hours

  • 📚 Morning (2h): Theory & Concepts
  • 💻 Practice (2-3h): Hands-on Labs
  • 🧪 Evening (1-2h): Projects & Documentation

⚖️ Ethical Hacking Guidelines

⚠️ Important: Legal & Ethical Responsibilities

  • Unauthorized system access is illegal
  • Always obtain written permission before testing
  • Operate within defined scope only
  • Never steal or modify data
  • Report vulnerabilities responsibly
  • Maintain confidentiality of findings
  • Follow responsible disclosure practices
  • Only test systems you own or have permission for
